🎙 LANITE — PROMOTIONAL HIGHLIGHTS & FUNCTIONALITY

Lanite is a premium, offline-first voice typing assistant for Windows that brings state-of-the-art AI transcription directly to any application you use.

🚀 KEY FEATURES

• 100% Offline & Private: 
  All transcription happens locally on your machine using advanced Whisper models. Your voice data never hits the cloud, ensuring total privacy and security.

• Global Voice Injection: 
  Type with your voice into ANY app (Notepad, VS Code, Discord, Email, more). Lanite injects text directly at your cursor.

• Seamless 'Hold-to-Talk' Control: 
  Triggered via a global Ctrl + Win hotkey. Hold to speak, release to type. It's friction-free and stays out of your way until you need it.

• Intelligent VAD (Voice Activity Detection): 
  Powered by Silero VAD to ensure it only captures your actual speech, filtering out silence and background noise automatically.

• Filler Word Removal: 
  Automatically strips out 'um', 'uh', and other verbal fillers to give you clean, professional text every time.

• Premium Bento-Style Dashboard: 
  A modern, high-end UI to manage your dictation history, view performance stats, and customize your experience.

• Scalable AI Models: 
  Choose your balance of speed vs. accuracy.
  - Tiny: Lightning fast for quick notes.
  - Small: The perfect daily driver.
  - Medium: High-precision for complex dictation.

• Visual Waveform Overlay: 
  A sleek, real-time waveform appears when you speak, giving you immediate visual feedback that Lanite is hearing you.

• Dictation History & Stats: 
  Track your productivity. See which apps you use most for voice typing, your total word count, and average transcription speeds.

• System Tray Integration: 
  Runs silently in the background. Access settings or show the dashboard with a single click from the tray.

🛠 TECHNICAL SPECS

- Optimized for CPU (Fast performance even without a dedicated GPU).
- Uses CTranslate2 for ultra-fast Whisper inference.
- Minimal RAM footprint in standby mode.
- Fully compatible with Windows 10 & 11.
